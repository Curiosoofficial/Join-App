About this project

Join is a learning and portfolio project from the web development bootcamp of the Developer Akademie. It is a non-commercial demo, not a real product or service.

Your data

This demo does not send any data to a server. Everything you enter (accounts, tasks, contacts) is stored only in your own browser (localStorage) and is removed when you clear your browser data.
Please do not enter real personal data or real passwords.
